# Core papers

This folder indexes the two papers used as primary sources for the ABIDE-DeepLearning coursework. The PDFs are linked at their authoritative hosting locations rather than copied into this repository.

## 1. BrainWaveNet

**Jeong, A.-Y., Heo, D.-W., Kang, E., & Suk, H.-I. (2024).** BrainWaveNet: Wavelet-based Transformer for Autism Spectrum Disorder Diagnosis. *Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention – MICCAI 2024*, LNCS 15002, 56–66. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-72069-7_6

- [Open-access accepted paper PDF (MICCAI)](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/paper/1241_paper.pdf)
- [MICCAI paper information and rights notice](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/112-Paper1241.html)
- [Official implementation](https://github.com/ku-milab/BrainWaveNet)

**Rights note:** The MICCAI page states that copyright and related rights remain with the authors or other rights holders. This repository therefore links to the paper instead of redistributing its PDF.

## 2. ABIDE-I

**Di Martino, A., Yan, C.-G., Li, Q., et al. (2014).** The autism brain imaging data exchange: towards a large-scale evaluation of the intrinsic brain architecture in autism. *Molecular Psychiatry, 19*, 659–667. https://doi.org/10.1038/mp.2013.78

- [Full-text article (PubMed Central)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4162310/)
- [Publisher article page](https://www.nature.com/articles/mp.201378)
- [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23774715/)
- [ABIDE-I dataset website](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/abide/)

**Rights note:** PubMed Central provides free access to this article, but free-to-read availability does not by itself establish permission to redistribute the PDF. Check the article's specific copyright/licence terms before copying it into this repository.
